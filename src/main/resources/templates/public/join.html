Organization Information

NACE codes classify the economic activity of your organisation. Choose the code(s) that best describe what your organisation does. Type at least 2 characters to search by code or description (NACE Rev. 2.1). Not sure which to choose? See examples and guidance.

How to choose your NACE code(s)

NACE is the European classification of economic activities. The codes you select describe what your organisation does and help other participants find and understand your organisation. Read more about NACE on Eurostat ↗

Search for your organisation's main product, service, or activity — not a legal or administrative category. For example:

  • A software company can search for "software" or "programming"
  • A recycling company can search for "waste" or "recovery"
  • A construction company can search for "construction"

You can select more than one code if your organisation has several activities.

Still not sure? Contact support and we will help you choose the right code.
