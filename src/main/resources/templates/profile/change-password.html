Password Policy

Your new password must meet the following requirements. They update as you type:

  • Minimum 12 characters
  • At least one uppercase letter (A-Z)
  • At least one lowercase letter (a-z)
  • At least one number (0-9)
  • At least one special character
    Any character that is not a letter or a number (e.g. !@#$%^&*~/'"\ or a space)
  • New and confirm passwords match
Security Tip: Use a unique password that you don't use for other accounts.
